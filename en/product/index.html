PLATFORM OVERVIEW

One platform for your revenue workflows

Kickscale works alongside your existing CRM. It captures and analyses conversations, prepares next actions, and makes information usable for coaching, deals and automations.

KICKSCALE · CAPTURE

Capture

Record and transcribe meetings, generate summaries and find the customer context you need.

  • Recording and transcription for Zoom, Microsoft Teams and Google Meet
  • Meeting summaries, AI search and a shared knowledge library
  • Foundation for coaching, CRM synchronization and deal insights

KICKSCALE · ASSISTANT

AI Assistant

Turn conversations into follow-up drafts, next steps and CRM updates, with team review where required.

  • Prepare personalized meeting follow-ups
  • Extract structured fields and propose CRM updates
  • Reduce manual notes and copy-paste tasks

KICKSCALE · COACH

AI Coach

Use call-based scorecards, examples and focused feedback to develop sales teams.

  • AI-assisted scorecards and qualification frameworks
  • Review real meeting examples and successful patterns
  • Support consistent one-on-one and team coaching
