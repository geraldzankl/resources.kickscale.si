About Kickscale

Kickscale is a measurable AI use case in sales with direct ROI.

Companies save >60 hours per month of manual work (≈ €2,000 per month per sales or customer success rep), increase closing rates by 5–22% and get reliable revenue forecasts with >94% accuracy.

At the same time, up to 99% of CRM data is documented automatically, with zero extra effort for the teams.

Result: more revenue, better decisions, less operational friction.

Products at a glance:

  • AI Sales Notetaker: 10–30% less manual work through automatic documentation
  • AI Sales Coach: 5–22% higher closing rates through data-driven coaching
  • AI Forecasting: >94% forecast accuracy for planning & steering
  • Insights & Trends: Clear management insights across all revenue teams
